Water heater repair in Austin: what it costs and when to call

Austin's hard water shortens the life of a tank-style heater to eight to twelve years. Sediment settles at the base, the burner works harder, and the first sign is usually a rumbling sound before a hot shower turns lukewarm.

Signs it needs repair

Discoloured water, a pilot light that will not stay lit, and moisture around the base are the three calls we take most often. Most repairs on units under ten years old cost between $150 and $450 in Travis County, which is well under the price of a replacement.
