They told you apartment income was for the chosen few. Turns out there was another door.

Brickfold is a members club that co-owns real, rent-paying apartments in Kampala. Some members own whole units, most own a slice, and every month, while the city pays rent, they collect.

Membership is not bought. It is applied for.

How to get in

You can own the unit, not the block.

Vetted members pool capital into a specific apartment. Ownership is recorded to the exact shilling and never rounded in the club's favour.

Rent lands monthly, split by stake.

Tenants pay, the club distributes. Your share of the month's rent is credited to your vault, proportional to what you hold.

Leave through the exit window.

Twice a year, members sell stakes to other members. No brokers, no waiting for a buyer of the whole building.
