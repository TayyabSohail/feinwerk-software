4.2 Multiple-element arrangements. Where a contract contains more than one performance obligation, the transaction price is allocated to each obligation on a relative stand-alone selling price basis. Where stand-alone selling prices are not directly observable, management estimates them using the adjusted market assessment approachF-11. Estimates are reviewed annually.

4.3 Cut-off. Revenue is recognised when control of the goods transfers to the customer. For contracts combining hardware and a twelve-month service element, revenue is recognised in full on delivery of the hardwareF-12, provided that the invoice has been raised before the period end.

4.4 Variable consideration. Volume rebates and early-settlement discounts are estimated using the expected value method and constrained such that a significant reversal is not probable. Rebate accruals are released twelve months after the contract year end if unclaimedF-13.

4.5 Contract modifications. A modification that adds distinct goods at their stand-alone selling price is accounted for as a separate contract. Other modifications are accounted for prospectively where the remaining goods are distinct from those transferred before the modification.
